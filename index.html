ACEH · INDONESIA · 2025

Two students. One filter. A real need.

After devastating flooding and landslides struck Aceh, a one-week challenge became a hands-on engineering project: help build a water filter that could be sent to affected villages.

01 / WHY IT MATTERED

When disaster disrupts everyday life, clean water becomes an urgent need.

In late November 2025, a devastating flash flood and landslide struck the Aceh region of Indonesia. Thousands lost access to basic necessities, including clean water.

A mentor from the Wings Peduli Foundation (WPF) told us that Aceh urgently needed water filters. WPF planned to donate ten units but was short on staff to build them. Together, we volunteered to build one.

02 / THE RESPONSE

From an existing design to a filter ready for the field.

With only a week before delivery, we spent an entire day at WPF working alongside its engineers. We studied how each component functioned, how the pipes were connected, and how the overall system was assembled.

03 / IMPACT

Three days from workshop to relief effort.

We completed the filter in three days. It was then shipped to affected villages in Aceh, turning a classroom-adjacent engineering experience into something that could immediately serve a community.

04 / THE HUMAN IMPACT

The moment the project became more than a build.

When we received a video of villagers using the filter, the project suddenly had faces, voices, and a purpose beyond the workshop.

The smiles and cheers showed us that engineering does not have to begin with a large invention. Sometimes its value comes from responding carefully to an immediate need and making sure a practical system actually works.

05 / WHAT WE LEARNED
“Engineering means turning practical problem-solving into something that can improve real lives.”

When a video arrived showing villagers using the filter, their smiles and cheers made the impact of the project tangible. The experience changed how I think about engineering: not as an abstract technical discipline, but as a way to turn careful problem-solving into something useful for other people.

— Jayden Katuari & Ian Tristan Yoncer
